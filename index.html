What I do and what it does for you.

A list of skills is no use to you on its own. So here is each one, and the difference it actually makes.

  • I design it and I build it

    The look, the words and the code underneath all come from one person. Nothing gets lost being handed between a designer and a developer, and there is only ever one person to ring.

    You get a site that looks right and works right, finished sooner.

  • It works on all devices

    Most people will find you on a phone — on the bus, one bar of signal, halfway through something else. They get the proper version, not a squashed copy of the desktop one.

    You get visitors who stay long enough to find what they came for.

  • I build the useful bits, not just the pretty ones

    Bookings, sign-ups, forms that land somewhere sensible, small tools that quietly do a job. If it saves you an evening of admin every week, it is worth having.

    You get your evenings back, and a site that earns its keep.

  • You get a person, not a ticket number

    I will tell you straight when an idea will not earn its keep, and be properly pleased with you when one does. Your project has my name on it too, so I care how it turns out.

    You get honest answers from someone who is actually invested.

  • A process you can see the whole way through

    You always know what is happening, what is next and what it costs. Nothing goes live until you are happy with it, and I do not vanish the day after launch.

    You get no surprises, no chasing, and someone still answering afterwards.

And that last one is not just me saying so

Let's get you popping.

Here is exactly how we would work together. No jargon, no mystery, and nothing committed until you say so.

  1. A handful of straightforward questions about your business and what is frustrating you — in your own words, in your own time.

    From you: about five minutes.

  2. A relaxed call about your customers and what you are really trying to fix. I will tell you honestly if I am not the right person — and who is.

    From you: 45 minutes. Still free.

  3. I look at how people actually use your site, what your competitors do, and where you are losing people. Evidence, not guesswork.

    From you: nothing. This bit is mine.

  4. One document: what I will build, what it costs, when it will be done, and what is deliberately not included. No surprise invoices, ever.

    From you: a yes, a no, or a question.

  5. Sketches, then designs, then the real thing. You get your own private page showing where we are and anything I need from you.

    From you: your thoughts at a few agreed points.

  6. I launch it, check it properly on real phones and computers, and show you how to run it. Then I am still here for a month afterwards.

    From you: somewhere to send the champagne.
