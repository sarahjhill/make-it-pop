Let's get you popping.

Here is exactly how we would work together. No jargon, no mystery, and nothing committed until you say so.

  1. 1 You get in touch You are here
  2. 2We have a chat
  3. 3I do the digging
  4. 4Plan & price
  5. 5You watch it grow
  6. 6It goes live
1

You tell me what you need

A handful of straightforward questions about your business and what is frustrating you — in your own words, in your own time.

From you: about ten minutes.

2

We have a proper chat

A relaxed call about your customers and what you are really trying to fix. I will tell you honestly if I am not the right person — and who is.

From you: 45 minutes. Still free.

3

I get to know your customers

I look at how people actually use your site, what your competitors do, and where you are losing people. Evidence, not guesswork.

From you: nothing. This bit is mine.

4

You get a plan and a price

One document: what I will build, what it costs, when it will be done, and what is deliberately not included. No surprise invoices, ever.

From you: a yes, a no, or a question.

5

You watch it take shape

Sketches, then designs, then the real thing. You get your own private page showing where we are and anything I need from you.

From you: your thoughts at a few agreed points.

6

It goes live — and I stay put

I launch it, check it properly on real phones and computers, and show you how to run it. Then I am still here for a month afterwards.

From you: somewhere to send the champagne.
