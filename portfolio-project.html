Muslim Bookers

Halal hotels and holidays in one place, with real reviews and honest prices — so nobody has to open six tabs to check they are not overpaying.

  Muslim Bookers   Web design & build   Responsive · Booking · Reviews

The Brief.

Booking a halal-friendly holiday usually means having six tabs open. One site for the hotel, another to check whether it really is halal, a third for reviews you half trust, and a fourth to work out whether the price is any good. It is tiring, and it is the reason people give up and book something they settle for.

The interesting part was trust. A booking site lives or dies on whether someone believes what it tells them, so reviews and recommendations had to be front and centre rather than buried three clicks in — and the price had to be something you could sanity-check without leaving the page.

The Approach.

Designed around how people actually browse rather than how a database is organised. Somebody looking for a family holiday and somebody looking for a quiet week away want different things from the same page, so the layout leads with the decision — where, when, how much — and keeps the reassurance close to it.

  • Reviews and recommendations sit beside the price, not three clicks away
  • Built responsive from the phone up — most holiday browsing happens on a sofa
  • Comparison made obvious, so nobody needs six tabs to feel confident

What it does.

Four things the site has to get right, because they are the four reasons someone abandons a booking and goes elsewhere.

  • Is it actually halal? Answered on the page, not buried in a policy document.
  • Can I trust the reviews? Real ones, from people who stayed, kept next to the listing.
  • Am I overpaying? Comparison built in, so checking takes seconds rather than six tabs.
  • Does it work on my phone? Built from the small screen up, because that is where holidays get browsed.
